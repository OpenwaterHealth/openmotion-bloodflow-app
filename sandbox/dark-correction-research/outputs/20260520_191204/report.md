## Recommendation

Continue deconvolution research for high-gain far cameras only. In this sampled phantom run, `deconv_fft` reduced camera 7 contrast-temperature correlation from `0.915` raw / `0.017` current to `0.002` and kept boundary jumps small, but it did not clearly improve mean stability and produced higher ringing than cameras 4-6. Do not replace production dark correction from this run alone; use this result to justify a deeper artifact/runtime pass and at least one additional scan.

Bin-wise subtraction should not be promoted. It required large clipping repairs on every camera and retained strong camera 7 temperature coupling.

The current moment-correction comparison produced very large contrast values in this prototype output. Before making a production decision, audit whether the prototype's sampled dark-anchor interpolation and the production `visualize_bloodflow.py` correction are numerically identical for this scan.
